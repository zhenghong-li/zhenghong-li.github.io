01

Research experience

ARPES with in situ electric and magnetic fields

Experimental physics

I designed and built a magnetic-field angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) system and collected spectra at fields up to 50 mT. I also designed an integrated electric- and magnetic-field sample holder and used COMSOL to model field distributions and photoelectron collection.

Experiment-guided AI-DFT agent

AI for science

I am developing an AI workflow that cross-checks density functional theory (DFT) calculations against ARPES, scanning tunneling microscopy (STM), and optical measurements. Experimental feedback guides calculation strategies and physical interpretation as the workflow expands from simple systems to complex materials.

FrontierPhysics

AI physics research benchmark

I contribute to benchmark task design and iteration, and review derivations, model assumptions, and numerical results in other tasks. My work evaluates AI agents' physical reasoning, inference from noisy data, and experimental decisions.

Related work submitted to ICLR.

Nickelate superconductors

Zhiyuan Future Scholars Project

I lead a project exploring substrate preparation and thin-film growth, supported by RMB 100,000 in research funding. I use AFM, RHEED, XRD, and low-temperature electrical transport measurements to characterize surface morphology, structure, and transport.
